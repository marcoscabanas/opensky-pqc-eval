These files were moved from the repository root without rewriting their contents. They predate the current separation of ordinary reception and delayed authentication; they are historical drafts, not the current implemented methodology. No editable manuscript source was supplied. Use [the current methodology](../docs/methodology.md) and [validated development results](../docs/development_results.md) for the next manuscript revision.

The revised editable [methodology.tex](methodology.tex) describes the planned
real-signature detached experiment: ordinary messages become ready at their
recorded times, while retained copies are grouped and signed independently.
Published hardware references are listed in
[methodology-references.bib](methodology-references.bib). The corresponding
implementation and assumptions are documented in
[docs/methodology.md](../docs/methodology.md), with runnable commands in
[docs/reproduction_commands.md](../docs/reproduction_commands.md).

The two one-hour datasets, acquisition provenance, final execution, analysis,
and publication figures remain pending. Existing development results are
historical validation artifacts and must not be presented as results of the
revised experiment. The original PDFs remain unchanged.
